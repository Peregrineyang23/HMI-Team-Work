## 2. Recognition Evidence Rule Applied

The v0.5 rule treats recognition evidence as stronger than raw activity counts:

- Customer written approval, acceptance, sign-off, or explicit positive feedback: high-confidence quality/adoption evidence.
- Manager/master-agent explicit written confirmation of quality, adoption, key contribution, or business value: high-confidence recognition evidence.
- Verified one-pass approval: strong quality/execution evidence if tied to review or delivery records.
- Figma/Feishu/Doc likes or reactions: positive signals, but not quality proof by themselves.
- Generic acknowledgements such as "收到", "辛苦了", "OK", or emoji-only reactions: collaboration/process signals only.
