## Insights From Self-Evaluation Updates

1. Additional evidence usually improved confidence before it changed the score. More files or sources did not produce automatic point increases.
2. Authorship and edit ratios improved attribution, but final adoption, quality, and independent contribution still required separate evidence.
3. Platform, method, and AI contributions earned extra-impact credit only after reuse, adoption, or measurable workflow value was verified.
4. Stakeholder approval and explicit manager recognition could unlock the upper end of a score range. Generic acknowledgements and reaction counts remained supporting signals.
5. Large self-score changes between submission versions mainly exposed earlier evidence gaps. Final calibration still depended on role expectation, impact, and non-duplication.
6. Manager-side records corrected blind spots in self-mined packages, especially for cross-project coordination, accepted delivery, and recognition evidence.
7. Short-tenure cases required output normalization by active period and stronger acceptance evidence for high bands; the whole score was not multiplied by tenure.
8. Render frames, autosaves, shared libraries, and version counts required deduplication to avoid volume inflation.
9. Manual source records and machine-mined metrics had to be labeled separately so reviewers could distinguish reported facts from derived estimates.

## Final V0.7 Rules

- Primary dimensions remain `50/20/15/10/5`; role differences change acceptable evidence, not the weights.
- Quantity signals describe scope. Quality, adoption, risk closure, reuse, and explicit recognition determine the upper score boundary.
- Every bonus or deduction requires a written basis and a non-duplication explanation.
- Short-tenure output volume is normalized by active months; quality, collaboration, responsibility, and acceptance remain fully scoreable.
- Self-evaluation agents produce evidence packages and recommended ranges. They do not issue the final manager score.
- v0.7 is frozen for 2026H1. Future semantic changes start from v0.8.

## Verification

All 11 final workbooks passed structural checks: score caps were valid, bonus and deduction basis fields were complete, formulas were clean, and totals were mechanically reconcilable.

## Privacy Declaration

Only aggregate statistics, anonymous learning, scoring rules, and desensitized templates are publishable. Person-specific workbooks, score snapshots, audit notes, raw evidence, private identifiers, customer/project mappings, and absolute local paths remain manager-private.
